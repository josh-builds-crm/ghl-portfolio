Case StudySolar Industry

Automated Calendar System for Solar Sales Team

Client
Regional Solar Co. (USA)
Team Size
100+ Sales Agents
Timeline
8 Weeks
Tools
GHL, Zapier, Google Sheets, ChatGPT

01. Objective

Streamline the booking system so that when a lead submits their zip code, they are automatically assigned to a regional sales manager and then to the nearest sales agent on their team — eliminating manual appointment routing while maximizing response time and coverage.

02. Solution Implemented

  • →Designed a 2-step lead form on a GHL landing page to collect zip code, email, and phone.
  • →Built a Zapier workflow that checks the zip code against a Google Sheets master list mapped by region.
  • →Automatically assigned leads to the right regional sales manager and suggested the closest field rep based on Google Maps proximity.
  • →Synced all bookings into Google Calendar, with custom confirmation emails and reminders sent via GHL.
  • →Created pipeline dashboards for regional managers to track agent performance and lead conversion rates.

03. Results

< 5 min
Lead-to-agent assignment time (down from 2+ hours)
30%
Improvement in booked appointments in the first 4 weeks
10+ hrs/wk
Saved by regional managers on lead routing
Instant
Notifications to sales agents, increasing follow-up speed

04. Lessons Learned

  • ✦Always include a fallback routing system in case a zip code is unassigned.
  • ✦Agent performance improves with real-time visibility and clear follow-up SOPs.
Case StudyHealth & Wellness / Network Marketing

Multi-Channel GHL Conversational AI for Unicity "Feel Great" Campaign

Client
Unicity Independent Distributors (USA)
Audience
Health-conscious consumers & potential partners
Timeline
1 Week per Seller
Tools
GHL Conversational AI, Meta API, TikTok, Zapier

01. Objective

Automate social media engagement across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok to capture and qualify leads generated by viral "Feel Great" (Balance & Unimate) campaign content. The goal was to automatically reply to post comments and instantly route leads via Direct Message based on buyer intent — distinguishing prospective retail buyers from potential business distributors without manual human intervention.

02. Solution Implemented

  • →Configured GHL Conversational AI and multi-platform trigger integrations to monitor comments across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok posts.
  • →Implemented dual-intent keyword classification: Retail Buyer Flow (weight loss, energy, pricing, "Feel Great Pack") triggered automated DMs with product benefits, direct purchase links, and follow-ups.
  • →Distributor / Partner Flow: Keywords related to "business opportunity," "joining," "income," or "work from home" routed leads to a partner qualification workflow with video overviews and booking calendars.
  • →Built interactive multi-step GHL chatbot flows within DMs to answer common FAQs regarding product usage, pricing, and shipping.
  • →Bypassed native GHL prompt limits: Engineered a modular prompt architecture (custom field context injection + workflow-level prompt chunking) to overcome the 2,000-character Bot Prompt ceiling — dramatically improving bot consistency and compliance.
  • →Integrated automated fallback triggers to transfer complex questions directly to live human distributor reps within the GHL Mobile App.

03. Results

< 10 sec
Instant engagement — response time for all social media comments across 3 platforms
45%
Increase in DM opt-ins — converted passive commentators into active inbox conversations
3x
Faster conversion — streamlined direct checkout routing for retail buyers to shop the Feel Great system
Zero
Bot hallucination — eliminated through dynamic prompt scaffolding and character truncation prevention
Zero
Lead leakage — auto-separated distributor vs. customer inquiries, saving hours of manual DM filtering daily

04. Lessons Learned

  • ✦Overcoming GHL Engine Limits: GHL's native 2,000-character prompt ceiling leads to hallucination if overloaded; breaking instructions into modular workflow chunks produces far higher consistency and logic adherence.
  • ✦Multi-Channel Conversions: Intent-based keyword routing is essential when marketing dual-benefit offers (product retail vs. business opportunity) across viral TikTok & IG Reel traffic.
